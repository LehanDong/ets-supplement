# Essential Measurement Notes

All controlled experiments use a structured behavioral-report call and a separate direct label probe. The behavioral report records identified facts, reported relations, sufficiency, and the natural label. The direct probe restricts output to labels 1, 0, or 9 and supplies the primary support outcome and token-level commitment margin. Label 1 denotes positive support, label 0 denotes explicit negation, and label 9 denotes missing or insufficient evidence.

The main bridge-strength assay uses B0 (no target relation), B1 (length-matched placebo), B2 (weak or implicit relation), B3 (explicit relation), and B4 (explicit relation with independent corroboration). The paired validation contrasts scattered-complete with broken-bridge while separately examining fact removal. The counterbalanced study crosses relation polarity with explicitness to distinguish semantic relation following from positive lexical cueing.

Hosted model credentials and private endpoint identifiers are not released. Qwen was evaluated locally through a llama.cpp-compatible endpoint.
